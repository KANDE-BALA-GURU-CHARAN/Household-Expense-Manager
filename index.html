How Totals Are Calculated

  • Income: Sum of Actual Spent on rows where Type is INCOME.
  • Outflow Budget & Spent: Calculated on EXPENSE, LOAN, and SAVING rows only, keeping income separate.
  • Available Balance: Income minus Outflow (Expenses + Loans + Savings).
  • To Be Paid: Sum of Budget on items whose Status is TO BE PAID.
